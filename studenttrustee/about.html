Nicholas “Nick” Bonasera

Student Trustee · Contra Costa Community College District

Marine Corps veteran Political science, Diablo Valley College President, Student Veterans of America DVC Chapter

I'm a political science student at Diablo Valley College and the elected Student Trustee for the Contra Costa Community College District, representing students at Contra Costa College, Diablo Valley College, and Los Medanos College.

Every one of California's 72 community college districts is governed by a locally elected board, and each board seats a student trustee elected by that district's students. The role exists so the people most affected by a board's decisions have a voice in the room when they're made. A student trustee sits with the governing board, takes part in its deliberations, and casts an advisory vote recorded in the minutes.

Before attending Diablo Valley College, I served six years active duty in the United States Marine Corps, beginning as an Amphibious Assault Vehicle technician and later as a Marine Security Guard at U.S. diplomatic posts in Tijuana, Khartoum, Johannesburg, and Prague. That service, and the transition back into civilian life, shaped my interest in veterans' mental health, public service, and the stories people carry with them. I plan to continue my education in public policy and law, working on legislation that improves opportunities for students, service members, and veterans.
