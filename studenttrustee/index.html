Find the student trustee for your district.

California has 72 community college districts. Each is run by a locally elected board, and each board seats a student trustee elected by that district's students. Search the map for your district, see who holds the seat, and which seats still need to be filled.

Find your district Legislative calendars

79 student trustees across 67 of the 72 districts. 5 districts have no student trustee listed. Two are confirmed vacant by the district (Ohlone, Rancho Santiago). Three publish no student member at all (Lassen, Victor Valley, West Kern). This information is up to date as of 22 September 2026.
